# Learn Biology: Membrane Transport Relations

## 1. Transport extends cell-boundary theory

Transport is the relation by which substances move across the membrane.

So:

**membrane -> controlled transport of substances**

---

## 2. Diffusion relation

Diffusion is movement from higher concentration to lower concentration.

So:

**high concentration -> low concentration**

This is a gradient relation.

---

## 3. Osmosis relation

Osmosis is water movement across a selectively permeable membrane.

So:

**water moves across membrane in response to concentration difference**

This shows that transport depends on both gradient and membrane selectivity.

---

## 4. Passive and active transport

Passive transport does not require direct energy input for the transport step.

Active transport does require energy input.

So:
- **passive transport: movement with gradient tendency**
- **active transport: movement against gradient tendency using energy**

This adds:

**energy input can maintain non-equilibrium distribution**

---

## 5. Example

If oxygen concentration is higher outside a cell than inside, oxygen can diffuse inward.

But if a cell must accumulate an ion against its gradient, it may use active transport and consume energy.

---

## 6. Reusable knowledge

We now have:
- concentration differences generate directional tendency
- selective membranes constrain passage
- passive transport follows gradient tendency
- active transport uses energy to oppose it
