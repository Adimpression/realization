# Learn Biology: Inheritance Relations

## 1. From gene expression to inheritance

Inheritance concerns how genetic information is transmitted across generations.

So:

**parental genetic contribution -> offspring genetic constitution**

---

## 2. Allelic variation relation

A gene may exist in variant forms, often called alleles.

So:

**same functional locus, possible variant forms**

---

## 3. Combination relation

In sexually reproducing organisms:

**offspring genotype = combined inheritance from parental sources**

Which traits appear may depend on how inherited variants combine.

---

## 4. Genotype and phenotype relation

A reusable relation is:

**genotype + regulation + environment -> phenotype**

---

## 5. Example

Suppose a trait depends on variant forms inherited from each parent.
The offspring's genotype reflects the combination received.
But the observed trait may also depend on expression pattern and environmental conditions.

---

## 6. Reusable knowledge

We now add:
- biological inheritance transmits structured information
- variation exists within shared organization
- phenotype depends on more than raw genetic presence
- combination rules matter in outcomes
