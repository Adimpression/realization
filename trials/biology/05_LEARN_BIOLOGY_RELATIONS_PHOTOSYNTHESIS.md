# Learn Biology: Photosynthesis Relations

## 1. Biological energy can also be stored

Respiration described energy release.

Photosynthesis gives a complementary direction.

So:

**light energy -> stored chemical energy**

---

## 2. Simplified input-output relation

In introductory form:

**carbon dioxide + water + light -> glucose + oxygen**

This is a transformation relation.

---

## 3. Relation to cell structure

Photosynthesis occurs in specialized structures, notably chloroplasts.

So:

**specialized organelle -> specialized biological process**

---

## 4. Complementarity with respiration

At a broad level:
- photosynthesis stores energy in glucose
- respiration releases usable energy from glucose

So:

**photosynthesis tends toward storage**
**respiration tends toward release for cellular use**

---

## 5. Example

A green plant exposed to light can use carbon dioxide and water to participate in glucose production.
That glucose may later be used by the plant in respiration.

---

## 6. Reusable knowledge

We now add:
- energy can be stored through biological synthesis
- organelles support specialized transformations
- one biological process can supply inputs for another
- life is organized through linked cycles
