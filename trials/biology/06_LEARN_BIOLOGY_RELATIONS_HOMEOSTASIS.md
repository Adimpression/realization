# Learn Biology: Homeostasis Relations

## 1. Life requires regulated stability

Many internal conditions must remain within workable ranges.

So:

**variable disturbance -> regulatory response -> restored range**

Homeostasis is regulated stability through correction.

---

## 2. Negative feedback relation

A foundational pattern is negative feedback.

So:

**deviation from range -> response reducing deviation**

---

## 3. Integration of prior lessons

Homeostasis depends on earlier ideas:
- membranes regulate exchange
- enzymes function best under certain conditions
- respiration provides energy for active regulation
- transport moves required substances

So it is a higher-order organization of earlier relations.

---

## 4. Example: temperature regulation

If body temperature rises:
- sensors detect change
- control systems trigger cooling responses
- heat loss increases
- temperature moves toward the prior range

---

## 5. Example: blood glucose regulation

When glucose becomes too high, mechanisms can promote uptake and storage.
When glucose becomes too low, other mechanisms can promote release.

So:

**same system, opposite responses, depending on deviation direction**

---

## 6. Reusable knowledge

We now gain:
- biological systems regulate variables
- negative feedback is general across scales
- regulation integrates sensing, signaling, and action
- many biology topics are intelligible as control systems
