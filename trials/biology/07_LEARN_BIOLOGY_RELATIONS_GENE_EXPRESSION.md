# Learn Biology: Gene Expression Relations

## 1. Earlier information theory now becomes more precise

Gene expression is the process by which information in DNA contributes to functional products.

So:

**stored genetic sequence -> regulated product formation**

---

## 2. Transcription relation

Transcription is the production of RNA from a DNA template.

So:

**DNA segment -> RNA copy relation**

---

## 3. Translation relation

Translation is the production of a protein sequence based on information carried by messenger RNA.

So:

**mRNA sequence -> amino acid sequence -> protein**

---

## 4. Regulation relation

Not every gene is expressed equally in every cell at every time.

So:

**same genome, different expression pattern -> different cell behavior**

---

## 5. Example

A pancreatic cell and a muscle cell may contain the same genome, yet express different subsets of genes more strongly.
As a result, different proteins are produced and different functions emerge.

---

## 6. Reusable knowledge

We now have:
- information storage is distinct from information use
- expression involves staged transformation
- regulation determines which products appear
- differential expression explains specialization
