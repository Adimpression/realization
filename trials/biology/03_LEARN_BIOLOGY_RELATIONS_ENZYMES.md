# Learn Biology: Enzyme Relations

## 1. From transport to reaction

Cells do not only move substances. They transform them.

An enzyme is a biological catalyst.

So:

**enzyme + substrate -> accelerated reaction**

---

## 2. Substrate specificity relation

A substrate is the reactant acted on by an enzyme.

So:

**enzyme identity constrains which transformation occurs**

Biological transformation depends on structured matching.

---

## 3. Rate relation

Enzymes affect reaction rate, not the basic identity of reactants and products.

So:

**enzyme changes speed, not overall reaction identity**

---

## 4. Condition dependence

Enzyme function depends on conditions such as temperature and pH.

So:

**enzyme activity = f(structure, substrate availability, temperature, pH, inhibitors)**

---

## 5. Example

If an enzyme helps break a nutrient molecule into simpler products:
- nutrient is substrate
- enzyme binds in a suitable way
- reaction occurs more rapidly
- products are released

If pH changes too much, activity may fall.

---

## 6. Reusable knowledge

We now add:
- biological processes often require catalyzed transformation
- structure constrains function
- enzymes regulate rate
- biological activity depends on environmental conditions
