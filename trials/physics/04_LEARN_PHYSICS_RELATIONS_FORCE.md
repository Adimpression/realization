# Physics Relations — Force and Mass-Acceleration Relation

This document teaches one reusable multiplicative substrate and then reuses it in force laws.

## 1. Reusable substrate

When one quantity equals one physical factor multiplied by another, the reusable family is multiplicative combination.

The reusable formula is:

$$ P = x \cdot y $$

Where:

* input: `x`
* input: `y`
* output: `P`

## 2. Force from mass and acceleration

Newton's second law is a multiplicative-combination law.

$$ F = ma $$

Where:

* input: `m`
* input: `a`
* output: `F`

This reuses the multiplicative substrate.

## 3. Weight as a special force

Weight is a force produced by gravitational acceleration.

$$ W = mg $$

Where:

* input: `m`
* input: `g`
* output: `W`

This is structurally similar to `F = ma`, but with the second factor specialized to gravitational acceleration.

## 4. Distinguishing the laws

* `F = ma` is the general mass-acceleration law
* `W = mg` is a special case under gravity

Do not collapse the general law into the special case.

## 5. One-sentence summary

Force laws reuse multiplicative combination, and weight is a determinate specialization of the broader force relation.
