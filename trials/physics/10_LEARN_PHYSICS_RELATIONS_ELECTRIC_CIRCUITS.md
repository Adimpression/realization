# Physics Relations — Electric Circuits

This document teaches one reusable driving-response substrate and then reuses it in circuit laws.

## 1. Reusable substrate

A system may exhibit a response quantity driven by a cause quantity and constrained by a resistance-like factor.

The reusable formula is:

$$ \text{response} = \frac{\text{drive}}{\text{opposition}} $$

## 2. Ohm's law

Electric current obeys:

$$ I = \frac{V}{R} $$

Where:

* input: `V`
* input: `R`
* output: `I`

This reuses the earlier driving-response substrate.

## 3. Electric power

Power in a circuit is:

$$ P = VI $$

Where:

* input: `V`
* input: `I`
* output: `P`

This reuses multiplicative combination.

## 4. Resistive power forms

By substitution from Ohm's law:

$$ P = I^2R $$

and

$$ P = \frac{V^2}{R} $$

These reuse earlier laws compositionally: the power law is combined with the current-voltage-resistance law.

## 5. One-sentence summary

Circuit laws reuse ratio, multiplicative combination, and substitution to connect drive, response, and energy transfer.
