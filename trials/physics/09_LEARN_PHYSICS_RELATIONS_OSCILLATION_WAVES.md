# Physics Relations — Oscillation and Waves

This document teaches one reusable periodic substrate and then reuses it in oscillation and wave laws.

## 1. Reusable substrate

When a system repeats a state after a fixed interval, the reusable family is periodic recurrence.

The reusable relation is:

$$ \text{frequency} = \frac{1}{\text{period}} $$

Or:

$$ f = \frac{1}{T} $$

Where:

* input: `T`
* output: `f`

## 2. Wave speed

A propagating periodic disturbance obeys:

$$ v = f\lambda $$

Where:

* input: `f`
* input: `\lambda`
* output: `v`

This reuses multiplicative combination.

## 3. Spring oscillation period

For a mass-spring system:

$$ T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{m}{k}} $$

Where:

* input: `m`
* input: `k`
* output: `T`

This reuses the periodic substrate, but makes the period depend on system parameters.

## 4. Pendulum period

For small oscillations:

$$ T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}} $$

This is another determinate law using period, but with different governing quantities.

## 5. One-sentence summary

Oscillation and wave laws reuse periodic recurrence, then relate repetition rate to propagation and restoring systems.
