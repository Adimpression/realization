# Physics Relations — Circular Motion

This document teaches one reusable inward-turning substrate and then reuses it in circular-motion laws.

## 1. Reusable substrate

When motion continuously changes direction around a center, an inward-directed relation is required.

The reusable relation is:

$$ \text{center-seeking effect} \rightarrow \text{curved motion} $$

## 2. Centripetal acceleration

For uniform circular motion:

$$ a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} $$

Where:

* input: `v`
* input: `r`
* output: `a_c`

This reuses the earlier ratio structure, but here the squared speed is related to curvature radius.

## 3. Centripetal force

The required inward force is:

$$ F_c = m\frac{v^2}{r} $$

Where:

* input: `m`
* input: `v`
* input: `r`
* output: `F_c`

This reuses both `F = ma` and `a_c = v^2/r`.

## 4. Distinguishing the law

Centripetal force is not a new basic kind of force by itself.

* it is the net inward force requirement
* tension, gravity, friction, or normal force may supply it

## 5. One-sentence summary

Circular-motion laws reuse earlier acceleration and force relations, but constrain them toward a center.
