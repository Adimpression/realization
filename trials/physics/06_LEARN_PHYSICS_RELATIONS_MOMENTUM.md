# Physics Relations — Momentum and Impulse

This document teaches one reusable change-through-interaction substrate and then reuses it in momentum laws.

## 1. Reusable substrate

An interaction can change a system quantity over time.

The reusable relation is:

$$ 	ext{interaction effect} = \Delta Q $$

Where:

* input: `interaction`
* output: `\Delta Q`

## 2. Momentum from mass and velocity

Momentum is a multiplicative-combination law.

$$ p = mv $$

Where:

* input: `m`
* input: `v`
* output: `p`

This reuses the earlier multiplicative substrate.

## 3. Impulse from force and time

Impulse is:

$$ J = F\Delta t $$

Where:

* input: `F`
* input: `\Delta t`
* output: `J`

This also reuses multiplicative combination.

## 4. Impulse-momentum theorem

Impulse equals change in momentum.

$$ J = \Delta p $$

This reuses the earlier interaction-causes-change substrate.

## 5. One-sentence summary

Momentum and impulse reuse multiplicative combination and connect interaction over time to change in motion state.
