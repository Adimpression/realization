# Physics Relations — Work and Energy Transfer

This document teaches one reusable transfer substrate and then reuses it in work-energy laws.

## 1. Reusable substrate

A system's energy can change because some process transfers energy into or out of it.

The reusable relation is:

$$ E_{\text{final}} = E_{\text{initial}} + \Delta E $$

Where:

* input: `E_initial`
* input: `\Delta E`
* output: `E_final`

## 2. Work from force and displacement

Work is a transfer law connecting force to displacement along the line of action.

$$ W = Fd $$

Where:

* input: `F`
* input: `d`
* output: `W`

This reuses multiplicative combination from earlier lessons.

## 3. Kinetic energy

Kinetic energy is:

$$ KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 $$

Where:

* input: `m`
* input: `v`
* output: `KE`

This reuses prior ideas of mass and velocity, but transforms them into stored motion-energy.

## 4. Work-energy theorem

Net work changes kinetic energy.

$$ W_{\text{net}} = \Delta KE $$

This reuses the transfer substrate: work is the change term for kinetic energy.

## 5. One-sentence summary

Work-energy relations connect applied force, displacement, and changing kinetic state through a reusable transfer structure.
