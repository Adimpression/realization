# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Participation in the Realized project must remain professional, evidence-led,
and safe. Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and implementations without personal attacks or harassment;
- state uncertainty, assumptions, and verification limits plainly;
- critique claims with reproducible evidence where possible;
- respect privacy, authorship, licensing, and responsible disclosure;
- avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or deliberately disruptive
  conduct;
- accept technical correction without retaliating against the person raising
  it.

## Scope

This standard applies to repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, project
communication, demonstrations, and events where someone represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect contributors or the project. Decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and free from conflicts of
interest. A maintainer named in a report must not be its sole adjudicator.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the channel described in
`SECURITY.md`. Do not publish personal information or sensitive evidence in an
issue.
